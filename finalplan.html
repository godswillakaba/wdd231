BlueEdge Landscapes Site Plan

Site Name

BlueEdge Landscapes

This name was selected because it reflects professionalism, reliability, and creativity in landscape design. The word "Blue" represents trust and quality service, while "Edge" suggests innovative and modern landscaping solutions.

Site Purpose

BlueEdge Landscapes provides professional landscaping services for residential and commercial properties. The website will showcase the company's services, completed projects, and contact information while allowing potential clients to learn about the company and request landscaping consultations.

Scenarios

  • What landscaping services does BlueEdge Landscapes provide?
  • Can I view examples of previous landscaping projects before hiring the company?
  • How do I request a consultation or obtain a landscaping quote?
  • What areas does BlueEdge Landscapes serve?

Color Scheme

Deep Blue (#1E3A5F)

Used for the header, navigation, main headings, buttons, and footer.

Green (#4CAF50)

Used for call-to-action buttons, highlights, icons, and hover effects.

Brown (#8B5E3C)

Used for decorative accents, borders, section dividers, and nature-themed elements.

Typography

Montserrat

Used for page headings, section headings, and navigation links.

Roboto

Used for body text, paragraphs, forms, and general website content.
